# Content Repurposing Prompt

## Purpose
Turn books into social media content for audience growth.

## Prompt
Break this chapter into:
- 5 TikTok/Reel ideas
- 3 YouTube video topics
- 5 quote posts
- 1 email topic

## Example
Output:
TikTok:
- “Stop proving your worth to people who already decided not to value you”

YouTube:
- “Why Over-Giving Is Destroying Your Relationships”
