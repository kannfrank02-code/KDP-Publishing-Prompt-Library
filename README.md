## KDP Publishing Prompt Library

## What This Project Does
This repository contains a curated library of AI prompts designed to streamline the self-publishing process on Amazon KDP. It includes prompts for book ideation, outlining, writing, formatting, marketing, and audience growth.

The goal is to turn ideas into fully structured, monetizable books using repeatable AI workflows.

## Who It Helps
- Self-published authors
- Content creators building digital products
- Women’s empowerment brands
- Coaches and spiritual entrepreneurs
- Beginners who want to publish books quickly without overwhelm

## Tools Used
- ChatGPT (prompt engineering and content generation)
- Canva (book covers, formatting, visuals)
- Amazon KDP (publishing platform)
- Google Docs / Microsoft Word (manuscript formatting)
- Midjourney (optional cover and illustration concepts)

## Example Prompt
"Create a 10-chapter outline for a women’s empowerment book titled 'Soft Girl Commandments: A Manifesto for Women Who Refuse to Chase, Settle, or Struggle.' Include themes of self-worth, femininity, emotional intelligence, and modern dating dynamics."

## Example Output
Chapter 1: Stop Proving Your Worth  
Chapter 2: Softness Is Power  
Chapter 3: Build Your Own Empire  
Chapter 4: Stop Romanticizing Struggle Love  
... (continues through full structured outline)
