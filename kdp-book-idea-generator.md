# KDP Book Idea Generator

## Purpose
Generate profitable, niche-specific book ideas aligned with target audiences and market demand.

## Prompt
Generate 10 book ideas for Amazon KDP in the niche of [insert niche]. Each idea should include:
- Title
- Target audience
- Core problem it solves
- Monetization potential

## Example
Input: Women’s empowerment

Output:
1. "Soft Girl Commandments" – Women tired of toxic relationships, focused on feminine energy and self-worth.
2. "The Healing Woman Journal" – Guided journal for emotional and spiritual healing.
